## Platform

web

## Users

- **Gate Officers & Security Personnel:** Performing rapid credential scans at KM100 training campus gates, workshops, and administrative buildings on mobile and handheld scanner tablets.
- **Campus Administrators & Training Directors:** Registering vocational instructors and staff, issuing official CR80 physical & digital ID credentials, tracking real-time on-site personnel counts, and auditing access logs.

## Product Purpose

The **KM100 Skills and Training Center Staff & Access Portal** is an operational gate access and staff credential management system. It provides instant badge scanning, cryptographic payload verification, real-time campus attendance auditing, and printable CR80 employee credential generation for KM100 Skills and Training Center, Inc.

## Brand Identity

- **Official Organization:** KM100 Skills and Training Center, Inc.
- **Brand Colors:**
  - KM100 Signal Orange (`#f37321` / `#ea580c`) representing vocational energy, technical training, industry readiness, and high visibility.
  - Industrial Charcoal Slate (`#181b20` / `#22262e`) representing engineering discipline, structural stability, and institutional authority.
  - Crisp White (`#ffffff`) for maximum contrast and readability.
- **Tone & Aesthetic:** Grounded, industrial, purposeful, anti-hype. Zero AI-generated decorative fluff (no floating mesh gradients, neon glow halos, or faux glassmorphism). High contrast designed for real-world readability under bright outdoor campus gates and indoor workshop lighting.
